VIT CHENNAI HOSTEL FACILITIES:
- Accommodation: 2, 3, and 4 bedded rooms with optional central A/C featuring fresh air supply.
- Health & Emergency: 24/7 in-house medical care unit, 24/7 ambulance facility, and tie-up with a reputed hospital.
- Fitness & Recreation: State-of-the-art gym, indoor games, entertainment halls with multi-channel TV, open-air movie screening for weekly shows, and exclusive visitors lounge.
- Living Amenities: Wi-Fi connectivity, centralized washing machine facility, snacks vending machines at all hostel blocks, saloon, daily housekeeping, and green ambience.
- Drinking Water & Hot Water: Treated RO & cool drinking water on all floors, solar hot water for bathing.
- Security & Campus Rules: 24/7 security vigilance. 100% smoking-free and ragging-free campus.
- Hostel Eligibility: Hostel facilities can be provided upon request for students residing within a 65 km radius from campus.

DINING & MESS FACILITIES:
- Mess Types: Special, North Indian, South Indian, Vegetarian, and Non-Vegetarian food categories served across spacious dining halls.
- Events & Canteen: Special festival menus provided on occasions. Separate campus canteen available.
